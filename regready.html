The framework

Four review levels, built to stack

Each level stands alone or builds on the one before it. Start at L1 to get files organized; run L3 when you need substantive regulatory testing; add L4 when findings need to become fixes.

File Assembly & Organization

Purpose · Completeness

Level 1 lays the foundation for everything that follows. Analysts gather, sort, and digitally organize every required document — promissory notes, loan agreements, collateral records, insurance certificates, title policies, appraisals, and financial statements — against RegReady's standardized checklist. Each item is confirmed present and legible; duplicates and superseded versions are removed. Anything missing lands in a deficiency log for immediate remediation.

Deliverable: Indexed electronic file Deliverable: Completeness certification Deliverable: Missing-items checklist Rack: $225 / file SLA: 2 business days

Documentation Verification

Purpose · Proper execution

Level 2 tests whether the documentation is actually enforceable. Reviewers verify that critical documents are properly executed, dated, and internally aligned — cross-checking signatures, corporate resolutions, loan approvals, and collateral descriptions to confirm the institution's security interest is valid. Insurance coverage, title commitments, and recorded instruments are examined for expiration dates, lien position, and endorsements. Irregularities are logged as major or minor exceptions.

Deliverable: Exceptions register Deliverable: Prioritized corrective actions Checks: Signature · resolution · insurance · title Rack: $400 / file SLA: 5 business days

Accuracy & Compliance Testing

Purpose · Regulatory consistency

Level 3 is the substantive regulatory and financial analysis. Each file is tested against federal and state regulation, internal lending policy, and examiner expectation. Reviewers validate system data — principal balances, rates, maturities, collateral values — and compare appraisal conclusions, loan-to-value ratios, environmental reports, and debt-service coverage against policy limits. Particular attention goes to Regulation O, Regulation B, the Flood Disaster Protection Act, and other applicable rules.

Deliverable: Examiner-style findings matrix Deliverable: Scored compliance summary Deliverable: Weighted exception index Rack: $900 / file SLA: 10 business days

Corrective Action & Advisory

Purpose · Remediation & process

Level 4 turns analysis into change. Senior reviewers synthesize findings from Levels 1–3 into a Corrective Action Plan that names the deficiency, its root cause, the regulatory reference, the remediation steps, and a target completion date. At the advisory level we can draft sample policy language, procedural checklists, or staff training outlines — then schedule a follow-up review to validate that the fix actually held.

Deliverable: Corrective Action Plan (CAP) Deliverable: Management presentation Deliverable: Follow-up review schedule Rack: $1,200 / session SLA: 10 business days

TRID insights

TILA-RESPA disclosure testing, line by line

TRID findings are expensive because they compound — a tolerance violation missed at disclosure becomes a cure, a refund, and a repeat finding. We test the whole chain.

Loan Estimate review

Required disclosures, fee descriptions, and delivery within three business days of application.

Closing Disclosure review

Final figures, cash-to-close, and fee tolerances reconciled against the initial Loan Estimate.

Fee tolerance analysis

Fees sorted into 0%, 10%, and unlimited tolerance buckets, with cures applied where required.

Changed circumstances

Documentation validated and revised LEs confirmed issued inside allowable timeframes.

LE / CD comparison

Side-by-side tracking to surface tolerance violations and calculate cure amounts.

Timing requirements

LE at least seven business days before consummation; CD at least three.

Delivery methods

Mail, hand delivery, or electronic — with proper evidence of consumer receipt.

APR & finance charge

Calculations cross-checked for consistency with Regulation Z.

Post-closing corrections

When a post-consummation correction is required, and how to document and notify.
