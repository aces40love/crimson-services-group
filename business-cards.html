Printer spec

  • Trim size: 3.5″ × 2″ (standard US business card)
  • Bleed: 0.125″ all sides → 3.75″ × 2.25″ artwork
  • Safe margin: 0.125″ inside trim
  • Colour: Crimson #8B1538 · deep crimson #6B102A · gold #C9A227 · ink #0F1B2D · paper #FCFBF9
  • Type: Crimson Pro (display) + Inter (contact block)
  • Recommended stock: 16 pt matte or soft-touch, full-colour both sides
  • Logos: the supplied brand files are JPG at 400 px. They are fine at the size used here, but ask your printer whether they want vector (AI/EPS/SVG) — if the mark ever needs to run larger, it will.
