What we do

From loan signings to transaction support

A signing agent's job is not just to watch someone sign. It is to know which of the 120 pages actually need a notarial certificate, catch the error before the package ships, and get it back to the lender in time to fund.

That is the difference between a closing that funds on Friday and one that slips to Monday — and it is why lenders and title companies keep calling back.
