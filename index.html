About Crimson Services Group

One holding company.

Crimson Services Group, LLC was founded to remove the friction from real estate and lending — by putting the services that normally sit in three different offices under a single, accountable roof.

Buyers, sellers, agents, builders, investors, and lenders all touch the same documents. When the notary, the title file, and the compliance review are coordinated by one team, deadlines hold, exceptions get caught early, and nobody spends a week chasing a signature page.
